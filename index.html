About Me

Namaste! My name is Mrs. Amita Garg, and I am a retired, professionally qualified Hindi teacher from India with nearly 30 years of teaching experience.

I dedicated my teaching career to Sarvhitkari Vidya Mandir Senior Secondary School, affiliated with the Vidya Bharati educational network in India.

Throughout my career, I taught Hindi to students from Classes 6 to 10, helping them develop strong reading, writing, grammar, comprehension, and communication skills.

After nearly three decades in education, I am excited to continue my passion for teaching by offering online Hindi lessons to children and families living in Australia.

My Mission

My goal is to help children of Indian-origin families calmly and confidently learn Hindi, communicate with their grandparents and relatives, and stay connected to their Indian cultural heritage.

I believe every child learns differently, and I aim to make Hindi learning enjoyable, practical, and meaningful.
